# Excel Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 750 sales transactions using Microsoft Excel to evaluate product, regional, and monthly business performance.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to present key business KPIs and sales trends in an easy-to-understand format.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends
- Track key business KPIs
- Create an interactive dashboard using Excel

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Formulas
- Slicers
- Data Visualization

## Dataset

The dataset contains 750 sales transactions.

The data includes:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Region
- Product
- Category
- Units Sold
- Unit Price
- Discount
- Sales
- Cost
- Profit
- Month
- Profit Margin

## Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |

| Total Sales | ₹753,637.49 |

| Total Units Sold | 3,020 |
| Total Orders | 750 |
| Profit Margin | 29.36% |

## Analysis Performed

### Product Analysis

Analyzed sales, profit, and profit margin across different products to identify high-performing products.

### Regional Analysis

Compared sales performance across North, South, East, and West regions.

### Monthly Analysis

Analyzed monthly sales and profit trends to identify changes in business performance over time.

### Profitability Analysis

Calculated total profit and profit margin to understand overall business profitability.

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard contains:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Total Units KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales & Profit by Product
- Interactive Region Slicer

## Key Insights

- Laptop generated the highest sales among the products.
- March recorded the highest monthly sales in the dataset.
- The North region recorded the highest regional sales.
- Total sales were ₹753,637.49 with a total profit of ₹221,240.22.
- The overall profit margin was 29.36%.
